The problem

Good MCAT prep costs over a thousand dollars. Students trust the official AAMC questions but find the explanations thin; they like the explanations in commercial banks but many cannot afford them. I wanted one free app that plans the whole study period, teaches from real textbooks, and explains every answer step by step.

What it does

Plans every day

Enter your exam date and free time; it builds a day-by-day plan with catch-up and rest days, and full-length practice exams on the way.

Questions in the AAMC format

Passage first, timer, flagging. Get one wrong and the explanation shows why your choice fails and why the right one works.

Flashcards that space themselves

Spaced repetition (FSRS) with basic, cloze, table, image and figure cards. Imports and exports Anki decks.

Built from real sources

Science from OpenStax open textbooks; reading passages taken word for word from public-domain classics and open-access essays.

Writes new questions

A generator turns a source section into a new question; several AI models and code checks each have one job, and any one can reject the item.

Private and offline

Desktop app for Windows and Mac. Study data stays on the student's computer, encrypted. A phone companion pairs to it.

Screens

Full window, no cropping. Select any image to see it full size.

Decisions I made

Cut a feature that looked great

The team built a photoreal 3D world for the home screen. It was impressive but slow and pulled focus from studying. I retired it after two weeks and kept a simple illustrated plan instead.

If it can't be traced, it's thrown out

Every question and card must point to the exact textbook section it came from and pass a check against it. An audit found errors in one source book; those sections were withheld.

Nothing counts as done until it's checked

A task is done only with a recorded test run and a second, independent AI reviewer that sees the change but not the builder's reasoning. Release rules are enforced by scripts, not memory.

Privacy first

No accounts and no cloud by default. Study data is encrypted on the student's own machine; AI features are optional and use the student's own key.
