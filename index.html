The problem

Quality MCAT preparation costs thousands of dollars, and that cost shouldn't decide who gets into medicine. Students trust the official AAMC questions but find the explanations thin. UWorld explains well, but many students can't afford it. This app offers both, at no cost.

What it does

Plans every day

Builds a day-by-day schedule from the exam date and the time available, with catch-up and rest days.

Exam-format questions

Passage-based questions in the AAMC format. Each wrong answer is explained step by step.

Spaced-repetition flashcards

Each card returns just before it would be forgotten. Anki decks import and export.

Sourced content

Science content comes from OpenStax textbooks. Reading passages are verbatim excerpts from published works.

Question generator

Writes new questions from textbook sections. Several independent checks review each one, and any of them can reject it.

Private by design

Runs locally with encrypted storage. Nothing leaves the computer unless the student turns it on.

Key decisions

Cut a feature that looked great

We built a 3D world for the home screen. It was impressive, but it slowed the app and pulled focus from studying, so I removed it.

Every fact is sourced

Every question and card must trace back to a textbook section. Anything that can't is discarded.

Nothing ships unchecked

Every change is tested and approved by a separate reviewer before it is merged.

Privacy first

No account and no cloud storage. AI features are optional and use the student's own key.
