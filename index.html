I'm building a free MCAT study app

It started as a flashcard app for myself. It plans your study, asks exam-style questions and explains every answer. I run the product and test everything. AI coding agents write the code, under rules I set.

Why I'm building it

Good MCAT prep costs thousands of dollars. That shouldn't decide who gets into medicine. Students trust the AAMC questions, but the explanations are thin. UWorld explains well, but a lot of people can't afford it. This app gives students both, for free.

What it does

Plans every day

You enter your exam date and how much time you have. It plans every day from now to test day.

Exam-style questions

The passage comes first, like the real MCAT. Get one wrong and it walks you through why.

Flashcards

Cards come back just before you'd forget them. You can bring in your Anki decks too.

Real sources

The science comes from OpenStax textbooks. The reading passages are real excerpts, word for word.

Writes new questions

It writes questions from the textbook. Several checks look at each one, and any of them can throw it out.

Private

It runs on your own computer, encrypted. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you turn it on.

Calls I made

Cut the 3D world

We built a 3D world for the home screen. It looked great, but it was slow and pulled focus from studying. I cut it.

No made-up facts

Every question and card has to trace back to the textbook. If it can't, it's thrown out.

Nothing goes in unchecked

Every change is tested and checked by a separate reviewer before it goes in.

Your data is yours

No account and no cloud. AI is optional, and it uses your own key.
